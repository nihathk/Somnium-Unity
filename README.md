# Somnium 🪞💭

> **🥉 3rd Place Winner - Aydın Youth Summit Game Jam**

**Somnium** is a 3D action-survival game themed around "reflection" and inner struggle. Players are trapped in a loop of fighting their own nightmares (clones), using the emotional power of their own words to survive increasingly difficult procedurally generated dreamscapes.

![Somnium Intro](<img width="2560" height="1440" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/de284303-83a6-499b-a2e5-fb4474a176fa" />)

## 🎮 The Core Gameplay Loop

The game blends fast-paced wave survival with a unique Generative AI / NLP twist:

1. **Dive into the Dream:** Fight against your own clones in surreal, procedurally generated dream environments. 
2. **Collect the Fragments:** During combat, defeated nightmares drop random words (e.g., *Independence, Joy, Hope*). You must collect 3 of these words before waking up.
3. **Face Your Reflection:** Wake up in your bedroom and interact with your mirror `[E]`. You must construct a meaningful sentence using the 3 words you gathered from the dream.
4. **NLP Emotion Analysis:** The game uses the **Groq API (NLP)** to analyze the sentiment and emotion of the sentence you typed (e.g., Anger, Joy, Enthusiasm).
5. **Return to Sleep:** Go back to your bed `[E]` to re-enter the dream. The emotion detected from your sentence directly alters your stats for the next wave—affecting your **movement speed, jump height, damage output**, and the weapons you use. 
6. **Survive the Waves:** Every time you go back to sleep, the "Dream Layer" (Wave) increases, the environments shift procedurally, and the enemies become much harder.

![Gameplay Mechanics](<img width="2560" height="1440" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/934a7149-76a7-4101-86cb-fad2a2a6d0ff" />)

## ✨ Key Features

* **AI-Driven Stat System:** Your character's physical abilities and powers are dynamically generated based on the emotional weight of your text input via Groq API.
* **Procedural Dreamscapes:** No two sleep cycles are the same. Environments generate procedurally, escalating in weirdness and difficulty as the waves progress.
* **Infinite Replayability:** The combination of random word drops, free-text NLP interface, and procedural waves creates a unique survival loop every run.

## ⌨️ Controls

* **[W, A, S, D]** - Movement
* **[Left Click]** - Use Reflection Power (Attack)
* **[E]** - Interact (Look at the Mirror / Go to Sleep)
* **[ENTER]** - Skip Intro / Tutorial
